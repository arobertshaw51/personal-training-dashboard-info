# Privacy Policy

**Personal Training Dashboard**

This is a private, non-commercial application developed for personal training and recovery monitoring.

The application accesses authorised Garmin and Oura information, including training activity, heart rate, sleep, HRV, readiness and other wellness measurements.

The application is intended to store and process this information locally on devices or private servers controlled by its owner. Personal health data will not be sold or shared with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Data is retained until deleted by the application owner. The owner may revoke API authorisation and delete locally stored records at any time.

Any future changes involving external hosting, data sharing or third-party AI processing will require an update to this policy.

For enquiries, contact the application owner using the email address provided during Oura developer registration.
